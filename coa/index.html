what's actually in it

A story first. A manual second.

"I don't think this is really an accounting book. I think it's a book about stewardship disguised as an accounting book. The accounting just gives it the language." — Kari

Part One
The Story
Thirteen chapters in Eggerton — truth, foxes in the night, and a rival kingdom undone by chicken fashion. Every chapter ends with Lady De's Debrief and The Court's Challenge.
Part Two
The Workbook
From whimsy to practicality: every character becomes a real, usable tool — balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, ledgers, aging, forecasts, ratios, controls.
