What's inside

A story first. A working manual second.

"I don't think this is really an accounting book. I think it's a book about stewardship disguised as an accounting book. The accounting simply provides the language." — Kari

Part One
The Story
Thirteen chapters in the Kingdom of Eggerton — truth, preparation, foxes in the night, and a rival kingdom undone by chicken fashion. Every chapter closes with Lady De's Debrief and The Court's Challenge.
  • The Weighty Egg · The Numbers Whisper
  • The Foxes in the Night · What the Ledger Holds
  • When Crestfall Falls · The Eggs We Count
Part Two
The Workbook
From whimsy to practicality: every character becomes a real, usable tool — balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, ledgers, aging reports, forecasts, ratios, dashboards, controls.
  • Core financial statements & record keepers
  • Receivables, payables, forecasts & budgets
  • Inventory, dashboards, controls & risk
